# Capability Map

> Urutan jelajah pengguna MVP. Detail AC authoritative di PRD §8 (`design/PRD draf 5.md`).

## Backbone

1. Orientasi/navigasi
2. Survey + persistensi lokal → Diagnosis
3. Diagnosis indikatif
4. Hitung mandiri
5. Baca roadmap statis
6. Eksplorasi pasca-MVP

### Orientasi/navigasi

- Landing pahami nilai/batas/CTA (US-01 Must)
- Navigasi lima halaman mobile/desktop (US-07 Must)

### Survey + persistensi lokal → Diagnosis

- Isi survey satu-per-layar F/M/R/A/C + konteks S/G (US-02 Must)
- Simpan `localStorage` berversi, redirect `/diagnosis` (US-03 Must)

### Diagnosis indikatif

- Indikator F/M/R/A/C tanpa lulus/gagal, bottleneck tunggal/“belum cukup jelas”, alasan + 2 langkah (US-04 Must)
- Verifikasi aturan diagnosis (US-08 Must)

### Hitung mandiri

- Margin/laba/impas/target dari input manual (US-05 Must)
- Banding direct vs platform (US-09 Should)

### Baca roadmap statis

- Survival/Improvement/Growth × 4 levers, baca-saja (US-06 Must)

### Eksplorasi pasca-MVP

- Rekomendasi AI (US-14 Future), chat roadmap (US-15 Future), mindmap (US-16 Future)
- Should: bantuan istilah (US-10); Could: transisi survey (US-11), skenario harga (US-12), ekspor/print lokal (US-13)
- Won't for MVP: akun/sinkron (US-17), kas harian (US-18), marketplace/payment (US-19), dashboard pendamping (US-20)
